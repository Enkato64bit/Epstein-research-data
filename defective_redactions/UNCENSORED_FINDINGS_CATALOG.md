## Technical Validation

### Recovery Method Verification

All text fragments recovered using geometric analysis:
1. **PDF content stream parsing** to identify text positions
2. **Black rectangle mapping** to locate visual redactions  
3. **Intersection analysis** to find text under rectangles
4. **Cross-validation** against DOJ reprocessed versions (no recoverable text)

### Source Authentication

**Original Files:** Text-based PDFs from DOJ multimedia court records
**Archive Source:** Internet Archive Wayback Machine captures (Dec 2025 - Feb 2026)
**Validation:** Docket headers, case numbers, filing dates match court records

### Comparison Analysis

**Original vs. Reprocessed:**
- Original files: 795KB (text-based with defective redactions)
- EFTA versions: 7.8MB (image-based with invisible OCR)
- Hidden content: Present in originals, absent in EFTA copies
- **Conclusion:** DOJ remediation was technically complete but originals preserved by Wayback

## Methodological Notes

### Extraction Limitations

- **Text positioning** may not capture all redacted content if outside rectangle bounds
- **OCR artifacts** in some fragments (encoded characters)
- **Page-level analysis** only — multi-page redactions may be incomplete

### Content Authenticity

- **High confidence:** Text extracted from original PDF structure
- **Medium confidence:** Dollar amounts and dates (OCR-dependent)  
- **Cross-validation:** Multiple documents contain consistent details

### Ethical Framework

This catalog contains **institutional accountability information** (financial flows, entity structures, legal coordination) rather than personal victim details. Focus is on:
- **Corporate structures** and shell entities
- **Financial coordination** between defendants
- **Evidence handling** and legal strategies
- **Property and asset** management

Personal victim information has been noted but not detailed for privacy protection.
